D09 — Cancellation / refund policy                        [BLOCKER]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Stage-based cancel/refund rules and agent refund authority limits
(with manager approval thresholds).
Owner: Founder/Support/Legal
Status: ANSWERED
Answer: Full cancellation and original-method refund before kitchen
acceptance. After acceptance but before preparation, cancellation may be
approved by support. Once preparation begins, customer-requested
cancellation is normally non-refundable. DAIG-caused rejection, duplicate
payment, non-delivery, wrong order or confirmed food-safety failure receives
a full refund. Missing/damaged individual items receive an item-level
refund. Support agents may approve up to ₹500 per order; amounts above ₹500
require manager approval.
